Question 59 (Updated July 2024 — Israel/Jewish Life)

Deutsch: Welche Städte haben die größten jüdischen Gemeinden in Deutschland?

Deutsch
✅ Berlin und München
○ Hamburg und Essen
○ Nürnberg und Stuttgart
○ Worms und Speyer

📝 Пояснение: Berlin and Munich have the largest Jewish communities in Germany today. The Berlin Jewish community has about 10,000 members, and Munich's has about 9,000. Before the Holocaust, Germany had a thriving Jewish population of over 500,000. Today, about 90,000 Jews are members of Jewish communities in Germany. Worms and Speyer were historically important medieval Jewish centers (the "SchUM cities," now a UNESCO World Heritage Site).

Question 66 (Updated July 2024 — Israel/Jewish Life)

Deutsch: Seit wann gibt es jüdisches Leben auf dem Gebiet des heutigen Deutschlands?

Deutsch
○ seit ungefähr 100 Jahren
○ seit ungefähr 500 Jahren
○ seit ungefähr 1200 Jahren
✅ seit mehr als 1700 Jahren

📝 Пояснение: Jewish life in Germany dates back more than 1,700 years. The earliest documented evidence is a decree by Roman Emperor Constantine from 321 AD, mentioning Jews in the city of Cologne. Throughout the Middle Ages, thriving Jewish communities existed in cities like Worms, Speyer, and Mainz (the "SchUM cities"). Jewish people have been an integral part of German culture, science, and society for centuries, despite periods of terrible persecution.
